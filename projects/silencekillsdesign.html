The Challenge

From 2014 to 2021, SilenceKillsDesign was my freelance company — and the answer to a question every small client asks: why hire four vendors when one person can carry the brand, the site, the marketing, and the media?

Over seven years the studio served more than 70 clients across hundreds of projects, spanning branding, marketing, advertising, software development, and audio/video production. The studio's name still carries my independent work today — it's the byline behind several projects on this site.
