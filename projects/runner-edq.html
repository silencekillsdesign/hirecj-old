Case Study

Runner EDQ

Five years as design lead for an enterprise data-quality suite — admin panels, end-user dashboards, and the sites that sold them.

Client
Runner EDQ — enterprise data quality software. The CLEAN_Data suite verifies addresses and contact data inside ERPs like Oracle, PeopleSoft, and Ellucian Banner, for customers from universities to enterprise HR teams
Role
Lead UX/UI Designer & Developer
Timeline
2015 – 2020
Team
In-house design lead, working closely with backend engineers, marketing, and sales

Product Design · SaaS Admin UI · Dashboards · Web Development · Technical Marketing

The Challenge

Data-quality software lives in an unglamorous place: inside ERP systems, used by administrators who configure integrations and end users who just need clean contact data. The CLEAN_Data suite needed interfaces that made both audiences productive — and public-facing sites that could explain enterprise address verification to buyers.

As the company's lead UX/UI designer and developer, I owned both sides: the product interfaces and the marketing presence — while also leading graphic design and marketing campaigns. (Runner's sister company, SurveyDIG, was part of the same role — it has its own case study.)

What I Did

01 CLEAN_Data Suite Admin Panels

Admins configuring data validation and address verification across Oracle-integrated systems needed interfaces that didn't require a manual to operate.

Responsive admin experiences for the suite — configuration, data validation, and address verification workflows designed to be self-evident. Customer savings of 30% annually were attributed to the combination of seamless design and the verification technology underneath.

02 End-User Dashboards

The people whose data was being cleaned — HR teams, registrars, advancement offices — needed visibility without admin-level complexity.

End-user dashboards for the CLEAN_Data suite, designed around what each role actually needs to see and act on.

03 The Sites That Sold It

Enterprise data quality is a hard product to explain — and the primary websites and campaigns were part of my desk too.

Designed and built the company's primary websites and product landing pages — like the CLEAN_Employee page above — plus the graphic design and marketing campaigns that supported sales.

The Outcome

30%Annual customer savings attributed to seamless design, data validation, and address verification

Five years covering the full surface of a B2B software company: product UX for admins and end users, the engineering handshake with backend teams, and the marketing design that kept the pipeline full.
