Case Study

Greenridge Naturals

A ground-up rebrand and e-commerce overhaul — 420% sales growth and 230% traffic growth in twelve months.

Client
Greenridge Naturals — a DTC wellness brand scaling fast on an outdated identity
Role
Director of E-Commerce & User Experience
Timeline
Dec 2022 – Dec 2023
Team
Solo designer leading a cross-functional team (Dev, Operations, Marketing)

Brand Strategy · E-Commerce UX · Design Systems · Technical SEO · Analytics

The Challenge

No design system, a 9-step checkout with 78% mobile abandonment, zero SEO metadata or conversion tracking, and shipping misconfiguration bleeding $4–8 of margin on every order.

As sole design and digital lead reporting directly to the CEO, I owned brand, UX, front-end, platform migration, SEO, and marketing in a 12-month ground-up rebuild.

What I Did

01 Brand & Design System

A legacy identity applied inconsistently across packaging, web, and social — undermining credibility at every touchpoint.

A rebuilt brand on a clean typographic foundation, systematized in Figma: brand tokens, product cards, collection grids, banners, and subscription UI — all responsive, all documented.

02 Site Architecture

Product discovery was a flat category list — no filtering, no search logic, no merchandising.

Catalog reorganized around three consumer need states — Daily Wellness, Targeted Support, Bundles & Value — with faceted filtering and best-sellers surfaced at the top level.

03 Checkout & Conversion

Nine checkout steps, no guest option — 78% mobile cart abandonment.

Checkout compressed to 4 steps with guest-first default. In testing, every participant completed purchase in under 3 minutes versus 8+ on the legacy flow.

04 Platform & Growth Engine

No structured SEO, no conversion tracking, and misconfigured shipping costing $4–8 per order.

Headless Shopify migration with Klaviyo, GA4 + GTM, ShipBob (corrected zone logic), and Yotpo — every integration mapped to a specific conversion or retention objective.

How It Was Validated

  • Moderated usability testing on the redesigned checkout (n=6)
  • Tree testing of new navigation — 94% found products within 2 clicks
  • Technical SEO audit; schema markup on 100% of product pages
  • Cross-device QA across 12 device/browser combinations

After Launch

  • GA4 + GTM event tracking across the full funnel
  • Klaviyo automations: abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back, welcome
  • Monthly SEO content calendar targeting high-intent keywords
  • Quarterly Hotjar heatmap and session-recording reviews

The Outcome

420%Increase in online sales YoY
230%Growth in organic traffic YoY
40%Reduction in shipping costs

Brand, funnel, backend, and acquisition rebuilt simultaneously — compounding returns no isolated fix could produce.
