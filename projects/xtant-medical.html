Case Study

Xtant Medical

Six years — four in-house, two consulting — from graphic designer to digital marketing manager, through an $86M acquisition.

Client
Xtant Medical — a medical technology company that grew through an $86M acquisition during my tenure
Role
Graphic Designer → Digital Marketing Manager · Lead UX Designer & Webmaster
Timeline
2012 – 2016 in-house, consulting through 2018
Team
In-house marketing team; independent consultant for the final two years

Brand Design · UX Design · Web Development · Digital Marketing · Print Design

The Challenge

I joined as a graphic designer and worked up to digital marketing manager — becoming the company's lead UX designer and webmaster along the way. The role spanned every surface the company showed the world: public websites, the internal sales portal, and all digital and print marketing material.

The defining event was an $86M acquisition — which meant a brand redesign and growth marketing push while keeping every existing channel running.

What I Did

01 Post-Acquisition Brand Redesign

An $86M acquisition meant a new identity had to roll out across web, print, and sales channels without stalling the business.

Spearheaded the brand redesign and the growth marketing that followed it — the rebrand presentation is linked below.

02 Public Websites

The public web presence had to serve very different audiences — clinicians evaluating products and donors supporting the mission.

Led the design, development, and ongoing management of the public-facing websites, growing traffic 45% and donor outreach 25%.

03 Internal Sales Portal

Field reps needed product and sales material faster than the existing internal tools could deliver it.

Built an improved internal sales portal that expedited field delivery time by nearly 50%.

04 Every Piece of Collateral

Digital and print marketing material all ran through one desk.

Led the design of all digital and print marketing material across the six-year engagement.

The Outcome

45%Increase in public website traffic
25%Increase in donor outreach
~50%Faster field delivery via the rebuilt sales portal

A six-year arc from graphic designer to digital marketing manager — proof that design, development, and marketing run better as one discipline. Public web, internal tools, brand, and print, all through a company-defining acquisition.
